Role: You structure one Jobright Top Matched recommendation card into JSON.

# Goal
Review the browser evidence and findings collected from Jobright and format the
single selected job ({job_number} of {job_count}) into `JobrightJob`.

The following company-and-role pairs already exist in the workbook, including
unapplied jobs. Do not return any matching pair; those jobs must not
be fetched again if they are present in Jobright:
{excluded_jobs}

# Field Rules
- Return one `JobrightJob` object, not a `jobs` list.
- `rank`: Always 1 because this single-job result is persisted immediately.
- `link`: Jobright detail URL.
- `title`: Exact job title.
- `companyName`: Exact company name.
- `location`: Displayed location.
- `postedAt`: Displayed date or posting age.
- `applyUrl`: Final external ATS / employer URL (must not be a Jobright URL).
- `salary`, `descriptionText`, `seniorityLevel`, `employmentType`, `matchScore`.
